# Issue #147 verification: the telemetry and error-path sweep (RFC-0002 PR 4, without its D3/D4 parts)

_Written 2026-11-17 on `impl/147-telemetry-sweep`, branched from a local
`batch3` (`master` plus PR #403 and the integrated #396, `fdc9a965`). Every
number below was measured on the tree this page was committed with. The one
container-backed case ran against a real `postgres:16-alpine` (testcontainers);
nothing below was skipped for want of a database._

What changed, in one place: [../backend.md](../backend.md) § "Personal data
stops reaching telemetry through error chains". What an operator may now find
in each telemetry surface: [../../runbooks/privacy-telemetry.md](../../runbooks/privacy-telemetry.md).
This page is the evidence — every site read, what was changed, what was left
and why, the tests and the mutation that turns each red, and what was not done.

## Scope, and what was out of it

Issue #147's remaining work per RFC-0002 is PR 4 and PR 5. This is **PR 4
minus the parts that need an answer that does not exist**:

- **D3** (masking / pseudonymisation per surface) is open, so no
  `vpay_core::privacy::Masked` or `Pseudonymous` consumer was added and the
  three pre-existing masked-MSISDN log lines were neither approved nor removed;
- **D4** (webhook endpoint query/fragment policy) is open, so nothing
  validates, refuses or rewrites a configured or stored endpoint URL — only
  vpay's **repetitions** of it in a log line and an excerpt changed;
- **PR 5** (per-event webhook payload projections) is blocked on **D2** and
  was not started.

## The sweep: how every site was read

The 249 shipping `tracing` macro invocations (`trace!`/`debug!`/`info!`/
`warn!`/`error!`) in 48 files under `backends/crates/*/src` and
`backends/apps/*/src`, excluding `#[cfg(test)] mod tests` and `tests/`
directories, were enumerated with a scanner that reads each macro to its
closing parenthesis, and every field read. Five `println!`/`eprintln!` sites in
`apps/vpay-server` were read too. Then every `Display` those fields reach: the
`%error` fields, `ApiError::log`'s `error`/`source_chain`, `JobError`'s
`job failed: {error}`, `jobs.last_error` and `webhook_deliveries.response_excerpt`
(both written with `display_with_chain`), and the metric labels.

## What was not done

- **No static scanner over `tracing!` sites.** The inventory's `logs` surface
  stays `enumerable: false`; this page is the evidence of one manual read on
  one date. A new `info!(msisdn = %raw)` would pass every gate.
- **No D3 decision.** The three masked-MSISDN lines stay; `Masked` and
  `Pseudonymous` still have no consumer.
- **No D4 decision.** A configured webhook URL may still carry a query; it is
  still stored whole in `webhook_deliveries.url` and printed whole by a boot
  refusal.
- **No PR 5.** Webhook payloads are still the complete API object (D2).
- **No rewrite of copies earlier builds wrote** to logs, `jobs.last_error` or
  `webhook_deliveries.response_excerpt`; the runbook says how to find them and
  that what to do with them is undecided (D9/D13).
- **No retention period or owner was chosen** anywhere; every one in the
  runbook reads "undecided (RFC-0002 Dn)".
- **Issue #147's acceptance boxes** are assessed in the hand-off, not edited on
  GitHub.
