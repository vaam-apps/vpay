In one sentence each. **The clean drain:** a payment settles, one of **two**
running workers claims its `payment_intent.succeeded` delivery, that worker is
sent a real `SIGTERM` while its POST is inside the merchant's receiver, and it
exits **0** having _finished the delivery_ — one signed POST at the receiver,
one delivery row `succeeded` with `attempt = 0`, and the same four-record
exactly-once invariant the two `SIGKILL` cases assert. **The timed-out
drain:** the same staging with `--shutdown-grace-seconds 2` against the same
6 s receiver, so the grace period expires with the POST still unanswered — the
victim exits **1** with `released=1` in its own warning, the job comes back to
the queue unleased, and a **restarted** worker finishes it, telling the
merchant a second time with byte-identical bytes. That second POST is the
point rather than a blemish: a timed-out drain is at-least-once at the
receiver, and until this case nothing in the repository said so.

**Determinism was the hard part and is not a margin.** The receiver's 6 s
delay sits strictly between `vpay_worker::webhooks::WEBHOOK_REQUEST_TIMEOUT`
(10 s) and `--shutdown-grace-seconds` (20 s); both comparisons are `const`
assertions in the test file against the shipping constants, so lowering either
budget fails the **build** rather than making the case flaky. And the case
reads the signalled worker's own transcript, rejecting any run in which
`webhook delivered` does not fall between `shutdown signalled; draining
in-flight jobs` and `graceful shutdown complete, exiting` — so a machine slow
enough for the receiver to answer first reports that, instead of passing
green. **Ten consecutive runs before this entry was written: 10 passed, 0
failed, 0 skipped**, 30.4 s to 99.8 s, the slowest three times the median and
still green — and **ten more in the review, on the rebased head: 10 passed**,
30.1 s to 53.3 s.

**And the constant is now pinned to the mapping, which it was not.** The two
`const` assertions above compare `RECEIVER_ACK_DELAY` — a number transcribed
by hand — against the shipping budgets; nothing compared it against
`slow-ack.json` itself, so the mapping could drift out from under the
arithmetic the compiler checks. Measured: `11000` in the mapping and both
constants untouched makes the case **fail in 20 seconds** with _"the worker
never logged `webhook delivered`"_, which reads as a regression in the drain
and is not one.
`the_slow_receiver_mapping_is_the_delay_both_sigterm_cases_are_built_on` is
the container-free guard that closes it, and it fires on that same drift in
**6 ms**.

**Four decisive mutations, measured rather than argued — the first two on the
implementation, both reproduced by the review, and two more on the case the
review added.** Removing the drain (`Drain::Clean` returned the instant the
signal is seen) fails with _the worker never logged `webhook delivered`_: the
send was cut off after the receiver had already _received_ it (WireMock
journals a request when it matches it and answers six seconds later, so
"received" is the exact word — the entry here said "accepted", which is the
one distinction this case is about). Removing `locked_at IS NULL` from
`vpay_db::Jobs::claim` fails on the double-send assertion, `left: 2
right: 1` — two byte-identical signed POSTs of one `evt_…` at the receiver.
Skipping `release_all` on the timed-out path fails the fourth case on
`released=0`; making the `Drain::TimedOut` arm exit `0` fails it `left:
Some(0)  right: Some(1)`.

The second mutation is why the second worker **co-runs** rather than
restarting: a worker spawned after a _clean_ drain boots only once the drain
is over, so the lease it must not steal no longer exists and the property
would be proved vacuously, on some runs and not others. **It is also the only
proof that the property is non-vacuous at all** — the co-runner's transcript
shows it never won a single claim in the whole run, and nothing in the
shipping loop logs an empty one, so no assertion can witness that it asked
while the lease was held. The mutation, which makes it ask and succeed, can.
The restart therefore lives in the _timed-out_ case, where there is
outstanding work by construction.
